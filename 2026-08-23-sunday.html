The Bitcoin ladder blew through, and Ben put a clock on his own lower low

2026-08-23 · sunday

Synthesized from what Benjamin Cowen and Jordi Visser published this week. They have not spoken to each other and did not participate. Quotes are verbatim and linked; everything else is labeled paraphrase.

Written August 23, 2026, covering material published August 17 through August 23 and market prices through August 23.

  • Ben: the Fed delivers one 25 basis point hike in 2026, September or December, then holds at 4%. If Bitcoin makes no new cycle low by December 31, he will not call a lower low into 2027.
  • Jordi: stay long Bitcoin above the 200-day. Three candles through it, a 22% week, and he will bail only if it loses that average. He still sees no September hike unless Warsh is outvoted.
  • The tape: Bitcoin hits 80,000 went from 23.5% to 87.0%. Gets down to 60,000 fell from 80.5% to 34.5%. The crowd that picked Ben last Sunday flipped to Jordi, then ran.

The pitch

Ben's strongest new line is a token hike, dated, then a hold. The August 17 video, The Rate Cutting Cycle is Over, is the one that belongs on the ledger. He names the size, the months, and the landing rate:

"We're talking about a token 25 basis point rate hike seems like a likely outcome to me."

— Ben, August 17, 10:01

"I think it'll either happen in September or December."

— Ben, August 17, 10:01

"I would argue it's probably going to happen this year and then after they raise rates, my guess is that they're then just going to hold at 4% for a little while."

— Ben, August 17, 18:32

That collides with Jordi's September-only no-hike claim. It does not collide with a hold in September and a hike in December. Paraphrasing the rest of that video: last NFP lost 23,000 jobs, unemployment is 4.1% from a 4.5% November 2025 high, and the 10-year is already back at 4.7% on its way toward 5%. The stock correction is still a 10 to 20% drop that does not start until September, maybe the very end of the month. Those are restatements of open IDs, not new ones.

The other new line is a time-stop on the lower-low stack. August 20, Bitcoin Rallies to the Bear Market Resistance Band:

"if Bitcoin's going to go lower before the next bull market begins, it'll happen before the end of the year, frankly"

— Ben, August 20, 5:28

"if we go for the rest of the year without a lower low, I don't think it would make sense to call for a lower low beyond that."

— Ben, August 20, 5:28

He has not abandoned a 2026 lower low. He has said when he will stop calling one. That is the claim.

He also owns the damage to red August, in his own voice:

"every midterm year, Bitcoin has gone down in August and right now it's actually up 10%. So what gives? Could Bitcoin go down in August? I mean, it's not as likely now as it was a week ago, that's for sure."

— Ben, August 20, 11:40

Paraphrasing the same tape: Bitcoin is below 70k and still down almost 50% from the highs, the rally from the low is about 20 to 22%, July 1 is the low so far, and they did get a daily close above the 200-day. He still wants a rejection at the bear-market resistance band. In Bitcoin: Dubious Speculation he puts the 2018 analog at mid-70s for this cycle, and he says if Bitcoin is still holding the 200-day in a couple more weeks the odds the low is in start to go up. That two-week test stays color. It is not a new ID.

August 18, The Market is Wrong About the US Dollar, is a restatement of the existing year-end dollar claim, with a 105 to 106 sketch. Color on 2026-08-05-cowen-02, not a second dollar row.

**Jordi's Sunday video, The AI Crypto Macro Nexus Point, is a process rule on the 200-day and a restatement of no September hike.** He is not adding a dated Fed claim. He is doubling down on the one he already has:

"there's no way I can see him raising rates unless he's outvoted. I just don't see how it's going to happen."

— Jordi, August 23, 19:07

On Bitcoin he will stay in the story until the average fails:

— Jordi, August 23, 3:03

The week he is describing:

"Three massive candles in a row breaking the 200 day moving average in Bitcoin."

The Treasury tape, in his words, not as a Funnel print:

"the Treasury announces increased sizes of nominal end liquidity support, the most important news of the week, and helped get Bitcoin moving. Then, of course, he couldn't stop there. Oh, could be more than four billion."

Paraphrasing the rest: gold had a 7% move back above its 200-day, which he reads as a debasement trade. The 10-year is a pegged wedge that they will keep managing, and he does not want the bet that it goes to 9 or 10%. His largest positions are now silver and Bitcoin. He cut AI in May and June. He still thinks the July lows are the lows, and he wants new highs in the thematic book by year-end as a grind, not a moonshot. Those last two stay color or restatements. They are not new IDs this week.

The synthesis

Last Sunday the crowd picked Ben on Bitcoin. Midweek it flipped. This window blew through. Hits 80,000 23.5% to 87.0%, biggest hourly +12.5 Friday 09:00Z. Gets down to 60,000 80.5% to 34.5%. Midweek already had the first leg (80k 30.5 to 51.0, 60k 77.5 to 57.0). Friday Z was the second. Do not invent a spot high from that jump. The files have below 70k, then mid-70s, a daily close above the 200-day, and a 20 to 23% week. That is enough to say the tape is with Jordi's breakout and against Ben's weakness-first order. It is not enough to grade the above-70k high.

On the Fed they have split again, and Ben put a real claim on it. Last Sunday Jordi's no-September-hike was aging superbly and Ben's old 57% number had collapsed. This week Ben dated a token 25 basis point hike to September or December, then a hold at 4%. Jordi doubled down: no hike unless Warsh is outvoted. The crowd moved a few points toward Ben (September 25bp 24.5% to 30.5%, any-2026-hike 46.5% to 55.5%) and still prices a hold. September 16 is now a head-to-head. December is Ben's second door.

The long end is no longer the clean agreement it was. Both men still want yields contained or higher for different reasons. Ben restated 4.7 toward 5%. Jordi now says Treasury will keep pegging the wedge, "could be more than four billion," and he does not want 9 to 10%. The 5.0% Polymarket line spiked to 43.5% intra-window and closed 23.5%, under last Sunday's 31% and under midweek's 36%. The shared mechanism is still live. The crowd that was paying up for 5% faded it.

The dollar is still the cheapest disagreement on the board. Ben dedicated a video to a year-end bounce, 105 to 106. Jordi spent Sunday on debasement after a 7% gold move. Nothing on the watchlist prices DXY. Both claims stay open and untrackable from the table.

Ben's new time-stop is the honest thing in his Bitcoin book. He has not killed the below-60k stack. He has said that if 2026 closes with no new low, he will not take that stack into 2027. That is how a book that is under pressure stays gradeable.
