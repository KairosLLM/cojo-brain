The tape flipped on Bitcoin since Sunday

2026-08-20 · midweek

Synthesized from what Benjamin Cowen and Jordi Visser published this week. They have not spoken to each other and did not participate. Quotes are verbatim and linked; everything else is labeled paraphrase.

Written August 20, 2026, following up the August 16 Sunday piece and covering market prices through August 20.

  • Sunday said: the crowd had picked Ben on Bitcoin, with the whole ladder sliding lower.
  • Since then: the whole ladder flipped up, and Ben himself owns that his red August call looks shaky.
  • The long end kept rising. 10Y reaches 5.0% is at 36%, the highest reading in the archive. Both men's convergence is aging well.
  • Jordi's debasement trade finally got priced. Gold gained four points and S&P lost four and a half in the cross-asset race.

What Sunday said to watch, and what happened

Sunday's cleanest line was the head-to-head: Ben wants a breakdown below 60k before stocks roll, Jordi wants the basement trade with Bitcoin catching up to gold and silver, and the tape had picked Ben. That read did not survive the week. Bitcoin rallied back to the bear market resistance band, and the whole Polymarket ladder moved up with it. BTC hits 80,000 went from 30.5 to 51.0, hits 75,000 from 50.5 to 70.5, and gets down to 60,000 collapsed from 77.5 to 57.0. The biggest hourly moves were Wednesday afternoon, the same day Ben published a video acknowledging the rally. In a week, the crowd crossed the floor and bet with Jordi.

Ben did not dodge it. His August 20 video, Bitcoin Rallies to the Bear Market Resistance Band, is the most honest thing he has said about his own call all year:

"If you look at midterm years, you can see that every midterm year, Bitcoin has gone down in August and right now it's actually up 10%. So what gives? Could Bitcoin go down in August? I mean, it's not as likely now as it was a week ago, that's for sure."

— Ben, August 20, 11:40

He is careful not to flip. The rally is up about 20 percent from the low, a daily close is above the 200-day moving average, and he walks the 2018 analog where Bitcoin sat at the resistance band the week of August 27 and then sold off. But the red August claim is now plainly on the defensive, and he says so in his own voice. The August 1 resolution date is now the most interesting line on the ledger.

The long end kept doing what both men said

The mechanism that held the Sunday piece together, the long end rising on soft data, kept working. 10Y reaches 5.0% rose from 24.5 to 36.0 over the window, with a single-hour jump of nine points Tuesday, and reaches 4.8% sits at 66.5. Sunday reported the 5.0% line at 31%, the archive high. It has since pushed to 36%. Ben restated the mechanism this week with a number attached:

"The 10-year yield is likely going to head higher. It's already back up to 4.7. I think it's going to head up to about 5%."

— Ben, August 17, 13:49

And he now frames the rate cutting cycle as over. His August 17 video, The Rate Cutting Cycle is Over, argues the hiking cycle has already begun overseas and that a token hike is coming here:

"The risk here is that the Fed actually has to raise interest rates before they can ever lower them again. We're talking about a token 25 basis point rate hike seems like a likely outcome to me."

— Ben, August 17, 10:01

The market is beginning to price the mechanism, if not the timing. Fed rate at end of 2026: 4.0%, the hike-and-hold outcome Ben describes, rose from 23.5 to 29.6, while 3.75% rose to 41.2. The September line is unchanged, still roughly 27 percent for a 25 basis point hike, but the crowd has started to price a hike by year end. That is exactly the shape of Ben's new call. Sunday's note that his Fed number had collapsed is being revised by the tape.

Jordi's debasement trade finally moved

Sunday flagged that the cross-asset market was still refusing Jordi's debasement trade, with gold stuck near 18.5 percent. That refusal ended this week. Gold wins 2026 rose from 19.0 to 23.0, S&P wins 2026 fell from 69.0 to 64.5, and Bitcoin wins 2026 ticked up to 13.5. The crowd is not yet on his side, but it is moving his way, and it moved the same week Bitcoin's own ladder flipped toward him. Sunday said the widest gap on the board was the market disagreeing with Jordi. That gap has narrowed.

Jordi's gold line gained ground in the cross-asset race. The synthesis

The week's honest summary is that Sunday's clean picture came apart. Sunday presented a crowd that had picked Ben on Bitcoin against Jordi. It flipped. The most useful thing this piece can say is that Sunday's read is not aging well on the crypto side, and Ben has said it himself. But the two men's shared mechanism, the long end, is aging better than ever, and Jordi's gold trade finally got some price. So the scoreboard is messier than it was: the bond market keeps confirming both of them, the Bitcoin market just moved against Ben and toward Jordi, and the cross-asset race moved toward Jordi too. The one line that keeps both men honest is that nothing has resolved. Ben's red August still needs a red close, and Bitcoin is one rejected rally away from snapping back toward his breakdown.
